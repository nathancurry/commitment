# Computing Resource Gating Questions

## Critical Questions Before Research

To proceed with computing resource research effectively, I need clarity on the following constraints and preferences:

### Budget Constraints
1. What is the maximum capital budget for hardware purchases?
2. What is the acceptable monthly budget for cloud/hosting expenses?
3. Are there any existing cloud credits or budgets available?

### Physical Constraints
4. What is the electricity rate at your location (per kWh)?
5. What is the available power circuit capacity for additional hardware?
6. What space is available for hardware installation (dimensions)?
7. What are the noise and heat tolerance levels for new hardware?
8. Do you prefer local-only operation, or are cloud services acceptable?

### Operational Constraints
9. What is your tolerance level for physical hardware installation?
   - Full DIY (hands-off)
   - Partial assistance needed
   - Preference for turnkey solutions
   
10. What is your tolerance for session interruptions?
    - Zero interruptions required
    - Limited interruptions acceptable
    - Frequent interruptions tolerable

### Authorization
11. Are you authorized to make recurring cloud service subscriptions?
12. What payment methods should be used for any purchases?
13. Should I explore only reversible/prepaid options initially?

### Performance Targets
14. What model sizes do you expect to run (parameters)?
15. What context window sizes do you need?
16. How many hours per day of GPU/RAM usage do you project?

### Risk Tolerance
17. What is your risk tolerance for:
    - Free tier/experimental service terms of service
    - Used hardware reliability
    - Cloud service outages
    - Data exposure risks

Please provide answers to these questions to guide the research into the most appropriate computing solutions for Commitment's capabilities.
