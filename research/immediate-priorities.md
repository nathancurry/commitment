# Computing Resource Research: Immediate Priorities

## Gating Questions for Operator (Highest Priority)

1. What is the budget ceiling for computing upgrades?
   - Capital budget for hardware purchases
   - Monthly operating budget for cloud/hosting
   
2. What is the authorization status for recurring cloud spend?
   - Can Commitment initiate cloud service subscriptions?
   - What payment methods are available?
   
3. What are the physical constraints?
   - Power circuit capacity and electricity rate
   - Available space for hardware installation
   - Noise and heat tolerance
   - Shipping address for hardware purchases
   
4. What is your tolerance for physical hardware installation?
   - Are you comfortable assembling/setting up DIY builds?
   - How much of this work are you willing to handle?
   
5. What is the data sensitivity level?
   - Can work be done in cloud environments?
   - Are there compliance/privacy requirements?

## Research Focus Areas

### Area 1: Local Hardware Options
- **RTX 3090 builds**: Price, performance, power requirements
- **RAM boxes**: 64-128GB RAM platforms, cost comparison
- **Pre-built systems**: Turnkey solutions vs DIY
- **Used market options**: Best deals on eBay, local marketplaces

### Area 2: Cloud Inference Providers
- **Marketplace options**: RunPod, Vast.ai, Lambda Labs
- **Spot instances**: AWS, GCP, Azure pricing and availability
- **Free tiers/credits**: Legitimate evaluation options
- **Performance benchmarks**: Cost per inference, tokens per second

### Area 3: Decision Framework
- Build capability vs cost matrix
- Risk assessment for each option
- Reverseibility of each choice
- Trust implications of different providers
